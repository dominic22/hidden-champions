# CLAUDE.md

## Project purpose

Build a curated list of billionaires ($1B+ net worth) — the “hidden champions” — and aggregate their assets, relationships, influence, and wealth sources.

## Asset aggregation

For each billionaire, collect:

### Core assets

- Primary residence (luxury home or penthouse)
- Vacation home or cabin
- High-end vehicles (sports car, luxury SUV)
- Investment portfolio (stocks, bonds, ETFs) — list major holdings first
- Rental properties
- Private club memberships (golf, yacht club)
- Fine art and collectibles
- Jewelry and watches
- Small business ownership
- Boat or sailboat

### Ultra-high-net-worth assets

- Multiple mansions and estates worldwide
- Private jet(s) and helicopter(s)
- Superyacht
- Sports team franchise
- Private island(s)
- Controlling stakes in major corporations
- Venture capital fund
- Skyscraper or commercial real estate portfolio
- Rare art collection (museum-grade)
- Racing team or Formula 1 team
- Private space company or rocket
- Vineyard or distillery
- Media company or newspaper
- Political influence and think tank funding
- Entire town or planned community

## Post-list enrichment

Once the curated billionaire list exists, expand into:

### Network & relationships

- Shared board memberships
- Co-investments and business partnerships
- Family ties and marriages between dynasties
- Shared advisors, lawyers, or bankers

### Wealth & finance

- Net worth over time (growth/decline trends)
- Primary wealth source (tech, energy, finance, inheritance, etc.)
- Tax havens and offshore structures
- Philanthropy and foundations (where money goes)

### Media & influence

- Media outlets owned or controlled
- Social media presence and reach
- Public narrative vs. actual behavior

### Geography & power

- Countries of citizenship and residence
- Where companies are headquartered vs. where they pay taxes
- Concentration of wealth by region or country

### Visualizations & analytics

- Graph/network map of connections
- Timeline of wealth accumulation
- Heatmap of political donations by party/country
- Ranking changes over time (rise and fall)

## Data sources

- Forbes Billionaires List
- OpenSecrets (political donations)
- SEC filings (stock ownership)
- Panama/Pandora Papers leaks
- LinkedIn / corporate registries
